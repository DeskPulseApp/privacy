DeskPulse Privacy Policy

Updated for version 1.4.1 · Effective date: October 22, 2026 · Applies to the DeskPulse app for Android and iOS

The short version: Signing in is optional. Without it, everything stays on your phone, as before. If you sign in with Google, your progress is saved to your DeskPulse account so you can carry on on another phone. We have no ads and no analytics, and we never sell or share your data.

What DeskPulse stores on your phone

Whether or not you sign in, the app keeps your data in its private storage on your device:

Your movement breaks (time, length, exercises, estimated calories, steps during the break)To show your history, streak, XP, badges and progress
Profile you enter: name, weight, height, age, sex (all optional)To estimate calories burned more accurately
Settings: reminder mode and interval, work hours and days, language, Pulse's looksTo remind you at the right time and show your Pulse

If you sign in (optional)

You can sign in with Google from Settings or when you start the free week. Then DeskPulse also saves the following to your DeskPulse account, so it can come back on this or another phone:

From your Google account: your name, email address and a Google account idTo create your DeskPulse account and recognise you when you sign in

  • Where: your account is stored with our database provider, Supabase, on servers in Ireland (EU). Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Each account can only read its own data.
  • How long: until you delete your account (see "Deleting your data" below).
  • Signing out keeps your progress on the phone and stops saving it to your account.

The 7-day free week of Pro

Each account and each phone gets one free week. Starting it needs sign-in. To check the phone, DeskPulse uses two values that are not your phone's hardware number:

  • a random id made by the app and kept in Google's Block Store on your phone, and
  • a one-way scrambled form of Android's app-specific phone id. The phone id itself never leaves your phone; it cannot be turned back from the scrambled form.

When the free week starts, these two values and the start and end dates are saved with your account. If you later delete your account, only the scrambled phone id and the day the free week started are kept, with no link to any account, so the same phone can't get a second free week. Nothing is charged when the free week ends.

Steps from Health Connect (Android) and Apple Health (iOS)

If you turn on step tracking, DeskPulse asks for permission to read your step count. It does not read any other health data and never writes to Health Connect or Apple Health.

  • Steps are used only to confirm your walking breaks, to credit walks you took without the app, and to avoid reminding you right after you moved.
  • If you allow background access, DeskPulse reads steps about every 15 minutes while it is closed, for the same purposes.
  • Step data stays on your device, also when you sign in. It is never transferred to us or anyone else, never used for advertising, and never used to determine credit or insurance eligibility.
  • DeskPulse's use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Physical activity sensor

On Android, the "Physical activity" permission lets DeskPulse read your phone's built-in step counter at the moment a reminder is due, so it can skip the reminder if you just walked. The count is compared and discarded; it is not stored or shared.

Calendar (DeskPulse Pro, optional)

If you turn on meeting-aware nudges, DeskPulse asks for read-only Calendar access. It reads only the start and end times of events marked busy, to hold a reminder until your meeting ends. It does not read event titles, descriptions, locations or attendees, and never stores, changes or shares calendar data. Turn the feature off or revoke the permission at any time.

Purchases (DeskPulse Pro)

DeskPulse Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or receive your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether your Google Play account owns Pro, on your device, so your perks unlock.

Notifications and alarms

DeskPulse uses notifications for break reminders (and, with Pro, optional eye and posture check-ins, and a reminder the day before a free week ends), and the "Alarms & reminders" permission so they arrive on time. These are scheduled on your device only.

Home-screen widgets

Widgets show your own progress from the app's local storage. Logging a break from a widget saves it in the same local storage (and to your account, if you are signed in).

Service providers

  • Supabase stores the accounts and saved progress for us (Ireland, EU).
  • Google provides sign-in, Google Play purchases and Block Store on your phone.

They process data only to provide these services. We don't sell or share your data with anyone else.

What we don't do

  • No account is required: DeskPulse works fully without signing in.
  • No advertising, tracking, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or files access. Calendar is read only if you turn on meeting-aware nudges.
  • No data is sold or shared for advertising or any other purpose.

Deleting your data

  • Delete your account: Settings › Your account › Delete my account. Your account and everything saved in it are deleted right away (except the scrambled phone id and free week day described above). What's on your phone stays. If you bought Pro, it stays linked to your Google Play account.
  • Can't open the app? Email deskpulse.assistant@outlook.com from the email address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my DeskPulse account". We delete your account and all saved progress within 30 days. See deskpulseapp.github.io/delete-account.
  • Delete what's on your phone: Settings › Backup and privacy › Reset everything, or uninstall the app. If you are signed in, Reset everything signs you out first and your account keeps your progress.
  • Export: Settings › Backup and privacy › Backup data shares your data as a file you choose where to send.
  • Revoke permissions any time in Health Connect, Apple Health or your phone's settings.

Children

DeskPulse is designed for adults at work and is not directed to children under 13.

Health disclaimer

DeskPulse offers general wellness reminders and estimates. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: email deskpulse.assistant@outlook.com.

الأسبوع المجاني لـ Pro (7 أيام)

كل حساب وكل موبايل ليه أسبوع مجاني واحد، ولازم تسجّل دخول عشان تبدأه. عشان نعرف الموبايل، التطبيق بيستخدم حاجتين مش رقم الجهاز الحقيقي:

  • رقم عشوائي بيعمله التطبيق ويتحفظ في Block Store بتاع Google على موبايلك، و
  • نسخة متلخبطة باتجاه واحد من رقم أندرويد الخاص بالتطبيق على الموبايل ده. الرقم نفسه عمره ما بيخرج من موبايلك، ومينفعش يترجع من النسخة المتلخبطة.

لما الأسبوع المجاني يبدأ، الحاجتين دول ومعاهم تاريخ البداية والنهاية بيتحفظوا مع حسابك. ولو مسحت حسابك بعد كده، بيفضل بس الرقم المتلخبط ويوم بداية الأسبوع المجاني، من غير أي ربط بأي حساب، عشان نفس الموبايل مياخدش أسبوع مجاني تاني. ومفيش أي فلوس بتتسحب لما الأسبوع المجاني يخلص.

الخطوات من Health Connect (أندرويد) وApple Health (iOS)

لو شغّلت تتبع الخطوات، التطبيق بيطلب إذن قراءة عدد خطواتك بس. مش بيقرا أي بيانات صحية تانية، ومش بيكتب أي حاجة في Health Connect أو Apple Health.

  • الخطوات بتُستخدم فقط لتأكيد استراحات المشي، وتسجيل المشي اللي عملته من غير التطبيق، وعشان مانفكّركش بعد ما اتحركت على طول.
  • لو سمحت بالوصول في الخلفية، التطبيق بيقرا الخطوات كل 15 دقيقة تقريبًا وهو مقفول، لنفس الأغراض.
  • بيانات الخطوات بتفضل على جهازك، حتى لو سجّلت دخول. مابتتنقلش لينا ولا لأي حد، ومابتتستخدمش في الإعلانات، ولا في تحديد أهلية الائتمان أو التأمين.
  • استخدام DeskPulse للمعلومات من Health Connect ملتزم بـسياسة أذونات Health Connect، بما فيها شروط الاستخدام المحدود.

حساس النشاط البدني

على أندرويد، إذن "النشاط البدني" بيخلي التطبيق يقرا عداد الخطوات المدمج في موبايلك لحظة ميعاد التذكير، عشان يلغي التذكير لو لسه ماشي. الرقم بيتقارن وبعدين بيتمسح، مش بيتحفظ ولا بيتشارك.

التقويم (DeskPulse Pro، اختياري)

لو شغّلت تذكير يراعي الاجتماعات، التطبيق بيطلب إذن قراءة التقويم بس. بيقرا مواعيد بداية ونهاية الأحداث المعلّمة "مشغول" بس، عشان يأجل التذكير لحد ما الاجتماع يخلص. مش بيقرا عناوين الأحداث ولا تفاصيلها ولا أماكنها ولا الحضور، ومش بيحفظ ولا يغيّر ولا يشارك أي بيانات من التقويم. تقدر تقفل الميزة أو تسحب الإذن في أي وقت.

الشراء (DeskPulse Pro)

DeskPulse Pro شراء مرة واحدة وGoogle Play هو اللي بيتعامل معاه بالكامل. إحنا عمرنا ما بنشوف ولا بنستلم بيانات الدفع بتاعتك. التطبيق بيسأل Google Play بس، على جهازك، هل حساب Google Play بتاعك عنده Pro عشان يفتح المميزات.
